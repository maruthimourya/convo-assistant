# Lost or Stolen Cards

If your card is lost or stolen, report it immediately through the app or by
calling the 24-hour line. Once reported, the card is frozen and cannot be used
for new purchases. Any transactions made after you report the card as lost are
covered by our fraud protection policy and will be refunded within 10 business
days. A replacement card is sent by post and arrives in 5 to 7 working days.

# Card Not Working

A card may stop working for several reasons: it has expired, it has been
frozen after a suspected fraud alert, or the magnetic strip is damaged. First
check the expiry date printed on the front. If the card is in date but still
declined, contact support so we can check whether a fraud freeze is in place.

# Daily Withdrawal Limits

The standard daily cash withdrawal limit is $500 across all ATMs. Premier
account holders have a higher daily limit of $1,000. The limit resets at
midnight local time. You can request a temporary increase for a single day by
contacting support before you travel.
