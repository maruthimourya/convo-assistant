# Declined Card Payments

A payment may be declined even when funds are available. Common causes are an
incorrect billing address, an expired card, a daily spend limit, or a security
block on an unfamiliar merchant. International payments are more likely to be
flagged. If a legitimate payment is blocked, you can approve the merchant in the
app and retry.

# Foreign Exchange Fees

Payments in a foreign currency carry a 2.75% foreign exchange fee on the
converted amount. The fee is shown separately on your statement. Premier
accounts are exempt from this fee on the first $2,000 spent abroad each month.
